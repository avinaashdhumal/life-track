Why LifeTrack

Most reminders whisper.
LifeTrack rings.

Ordinary notifications get silenced by battery savers and swiped away by accident. A missed dose shouldn't depend on luck, so LifeTrack uses the same system alarm clock your phone's own alarm uses.

Rings like an alarm clock

A full-screen alarm over the lock screen with big Taken, Snooze and Skip buttons. It keeps working in Doze mode and comes back after a restart.

Guards against mistakes

Warns you if a dose is too soon after the last one or over the daily maximum. Counts your tablets and tells you before the strip runs out.

Follows your day

Set doses by the clock or by your routine: "with lunch", "30 minutes after waking". Change your lunch time and the reminders move with it. Shift work is built in.

For every age

One app for the whole family.

From a grandparent's blood-pressure tablet to a student's inhaler, LifeTrack fits the way each person actually lives.

65+

Parents & grandparents

A loud, full-screen alarm and one big button to say "Taken". The app speaks Hindi and Marathi, and keeps blood group and an emergency contact on the profile.

30–60

The family caregiver

Set it up once on a parent's phone. Get a refill warning before the medicine runs out, track a child's syrup with a daily limit, and back everything up when they change phones.

22–50

Shift workers & busy days

Nurses, guards, factory and IT teams: build a rotation like "5 mornings, 2 off, 5 nights" and see 90 days ahead. A heads-up arrives the evening before your shift changes.

Set up in a minute

Three steps. Then just live.

  1. 1

    Install & allow

    Install the app and allow notifications and alarms. The built-in Health Check shows a green tick when your phone is ready.

  2. 2

    Add a medicine

    Name, strength, and when: fixed times, "with dinner", or only when needed. Add how many tablets you have.

  3. 3

    It rings. Tap Taken.

    Right from the lock screen. LifeTrack records the dose, counts down your stock, and lines up the next one.

Questions

Good to know.

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no subscription, no paid features. LifeTrack is open source under the MIT license.

Does it need the internet?

No. Everything works offline, and the app never sends your data anywhere.

Why isn't it on the Play Store?

Not yet. For now you install the APK directly. Android shows a warning for any app from outside the Play Store, and the install steps above walk you through it.

Will the alarm ring if my phone is locked or on battery saver?

That's what it's built for. It uses Android's alarm-clock system, the same one your phone's alarm uses. Some phones (Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo, Realme, Samsung) also need Autostart and Unrestricted battery turned on, and the in-app Health Check shows you where.

Can I use it for someone else, like my mother?

Yes. Install it on her phone, add her medicines once, and choose Hindi or Marathi if she prefers. The profile keeps her blood group and an emergency contact.

What happens to my data if I change phones?

Make a backup from Profile, move the file to the new phone, and restore it. Uninstalling the app deletes its data, so back up first.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. The code is ready to grow into iOS later.
